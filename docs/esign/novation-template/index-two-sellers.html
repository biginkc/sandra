Addendum to purchase and sale agreement

Equity Buyer Program ("EBP") addendum

The undersigned parties to a Purchase and Sale Agreement dated by and between ("Buyer") and ("Seller") for the purchase and sale of that certain property municipally known ("Property"), hereby mutually agree to amend said Agreement as follows:

Seller has agreed to opt-in for the Equity Buyer's Program ("EBP"). EBP is a Seller assist program designed to work with buyers using both conventional and non-conventional loans. Seller can be paid more money to participate in EBP due to closing with a loan which may require repairs, appraisals, inspections, commissions, concessions, and other fees, that will be paid for or facilitated through the Equity Buyer's Program (EBP) and/or Retail Buyer. It isn't uncommon for the retail buyer in EBP to agree to pay 80-100% of the appraised value of Property. Seller will not incur any additional fees. Both parties agree to extend the closing timeline to adhere to any broker or loan requirements for EBP/retail buyer's loan to close. The target closing will take place after Seller has provided Buyer access to the property, and the property is listed on the MLS. The target closing is typically on or before 45-60 business days, but can be delayed due to bank, loan, or Property specific reasons. Property to be broom swept at move out.

All other terms and conditions of the Purchase Contract to remain the same.
