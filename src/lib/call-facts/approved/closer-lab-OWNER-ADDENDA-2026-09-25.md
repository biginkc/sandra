# Owner addenda — approved 2026-09-25

Approved by the owner in chat ("Approve all four, bare no rush counts"). These override or extend the 2026-09-21 category definitions. Never hand-edit; a new approval replaces this file.

### bad experience

Did the seller bring up a bad experience, past or present, with a real estate deal or buyer?

Yes: they describe what went wrong with another deal, agent, or investor.

No: doubt aimed at this company's honesty is trust, not this.

### not rushed

Did the seller say the sale can wait 90 or more days, or that they have no timeline?

Yes: a 90-plus-day horizon, an open-ended "whenever," or a bare "no rush."

No: a deadline under 90 days is not this.

### motivation

Did the seller state why they are selling, or a pressure behind it, whether financial, emotional, or situational?

Yes: any stated reason or pressure. Not an objection.

No: a question or a hindrance is an objection, even if it also explains why they're selling.

### objection

Did the seller ask a question, or name something that must be answered before the deal moves forward?

Yes: the rep could not simply move on without responding.

No: a reason for selling, a plain answer to the rep's question, or agreement.
