# Owner binary objection questions

Answer each question yes or no from the seller's words. Apply the exclusion rules in the frozen rubric when two labels describe the same concern.

1. **Decision time:** Does the seller need more time to decide whether to accept or sign?
2. **Housing delay:** Does the seller need time to find somewhere to live before selling?
3. **Consult another person:** Does the seller need another person’s input or approval before deciding or signing?
4. **Personal agreement review:** Does the seller need time to read the purchase agreement themselves?
5. **Sale in 90+ days:** Does the seller want the sale or closing to happen at least 90 days from now?
6. **Other question or concern:** Does the seller raise a question or concern that no named category covers?
7. **Trust:** Does the seller question whether this call or company is legitimate or honest?
8. **Funding or earnest money:** Does the seller question the buyer’s ability to pay, request proof of funds, or require earnest money?
9. **Offered-price pushback:** Does the seller reject, criticize, or ask to increase a purchase price already offered?
10. **Listing alternative:** Does the seller propose using a realtor or listing instead of this cash-sale option?
11. **Outside valuation:** Does the seller state a numeric estimate of the property’s worth that is not a buyer’s offer?
12. **Buyer identity:** Does the seller ask or question who will actually buy or take title to the property?
13. **Property access:** Does the seller question the need for a property visit or resist allowing one?
14. **Initial offer request:** Does the seller ask what this company will pay before it has given a purchase price?
15. **Offer-now condition:** Does the seller make getting a number or offer now a condition of continuing?
16. **Attorney review:** Does the seller want an attorney or legal representative to review a document for this deal?
17. **Closing certainty:** Does the seller question whether this buyer will actually close or honor a closing commitment?
18. **Switch to text:** Does the seller ask to use text instead of continuing the phone conversation?
19. **Busy or callback:** Does the seller say they cannot talk now or ask to stop or reschedule because they are unavailable?
20. **Sight-unseen explanation:** Does the seller ask how an offer can be made without seeing the property?
21. **Closing timing:** Does the seller ask how long closing takes or whether a closing date is possible?
22. **Competing offer:** Does the seller mention an actual offer received from another buyer?
23. **Transaction process:** Does the seller ask how the sale works or what steps happen next?
24. **Email refusal:** Does the seller decline, avoid, or say they cannot provide an email address for this deal?
25. **Company compensation:** Does the seller ask about or object to how this company or representative gets paid?
26. **Contract meaning:** Does the seller ask what a contract term means or what it legally requires or allows?
27. **Seller costs:** Does the seller ask about or object to fees or transaction costs they must pay?
28. **Offer calculation:** Does the seller ask about or challenge how the offer amount is calculated?
29. **Offer changes:** Does the seller question or resist a later reduction or change to an offer?
30. **Property preparation:** Does the seller ask about or resist cleaning, repairs, or removing belongings for a visit or sale?
